About Us

Read More
In the heart of Campeche’s historic center lies Casa Don Gustavo, a boutique hotel housed in a magnificent 17th-century colonial mansion that has been carefully restored to preserve its original character. More than just a hotel, Casa Don Gustavo is a place where art and culture converge—a space where architectural heritage meets the city's contemporary creativity. During their stay, guests can discover exhibitions by local artists, small galleries, creative workshops, literary gatherings, and intimate artistic performances, transforming their travel experience into an authentic encounter with Campeche’s culture.The guest rooms retain the charm of colonial architecture, blending traditional furnishings and artisanal details with the comforts of a boutique hotel. The interior courtyard, historic salons, and common areas serve as natural settings for both artistic expression and relaxation. Casa Don Gustavo invites guests to experience Campeche in a unique way: not merely visiting its history, but becoming part of its cultural life.Nearby attractions invite you to take a walk around Casa Don Gustavo to enjoy the Historic Center of Campeche, Museums of Mayan Culture, Bastions, Walls, the imposing Cathedral of Campeche, and a short distance from the Convention Center and House of Crafts Tukulná. Besides being the meeting point for outings to Archaeological Cities such as Edzná and Calakmul. Welcome to the pleasure of living!
