Underwater Cenote Photoshoot in the Riviera Maya

No experience needed

Private underwater portraits in the cenotes between Tulum and Playa del Carmen — fully guided from start to finish, even if you’ve never done anything like this before.

SEE THE EXPERIENCE

A Private Experience, Not Just a Photoshoot

This isn’t a group tour with a camera pointed at you for two minutes. It’s a private session, built around you — your comfort, your pace, your story.

You don’t come out with a set of vacation pictures. You come out with images that actually look like you, taken in one of the most striking natural settings in Mexico.

Choose your experience

More than one way to experience the Riviera Maya:
from underwater cenotes, to the beach, each session is designed around the environment, the light and the person in front of my camera.

Underwater Photoshoot

Private underwater portraits inside a cenote, fully submerged, using natural light filtering through the water. This is the signature experience — the one most people come to me for.

Cenote Jungle Photoshoot

For those who love the cenote atmosphere but prefer to stay above the water. Portraits at the surface, half-in half-out, or surrounded by jungle — same artistic result, without going fully underwater.

Explore

Diving Photography

Professional photography during your dive, in the open sea or in a cenote. I document the moments you can’t see from above — your movement, the marine life, the light.

Explore

Beach Photography

Relaxed portraits by the Caribbean Sea in Playa del Carmen. Couple sessions, solo portraits, editorial-style photography — no water required.

First time underwater ?


You don’t need any previous experience.

If you’ve never done an underwater photoshoot before, you don’t have to know how to pose, hold your breath or move underwater.

I guide you step by step through breathing, body position, movement and posing, so you can gradually feel comfortable in the water while I focus on creating the images.

Because the session is private, there’s no group to keep up with and no pressure to get everything right on the first try.

What makes this experience different?

Private, calm, and planned around the cenote.

Every session is private and planned around the natural conditions of the cenote — including light, water clarity and timing.

There’s no group to follow and no need to rush from one setup to another. We work at a pace that allows you to relax, adjust and make the most of the environment.

How it works?

  • Choose your date
    We select the best day based on availability and conditions.

  • Get prepared
    I’ll guide you before the session on what to bring, what to wear, and what to expect.

  • The photoshoot
    During the session, I guide you step by step in the water so you can feel comfortable and move naturally.

  • Receive your images
    After the session, you’ll receive a curated selection of professionally edited photos.
