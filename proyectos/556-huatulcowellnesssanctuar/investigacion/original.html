FAQ

Nervous system regulation coaching — common questions

Where do I start?
Start with Bio-Audit™ ($249). It maps physical stress patterns, allostatic load markers, adrenal exhaustion signals, vagal tone context, and the safest next step before any deeper work begins. Most clients route from Bio-Audit™ into Executive Burnout Recovery ($3,800) if burnout is the dominant pattern.
What is the difference between somatic coaching and talk-based support?
Talk-based support works primarily at the cognitive and emotional level — it processes the story. Somatic coaching works with the autonomic nervous system — the body's actual stress-response pattern, vagal tone, HPA axis rhythm, and regulation capacity. Natoorales is coaching and education only. These approaches can be complementary when appropriate.
What is executive burnout recovery coaching?
Executive Burnout Recovery is private 1:1 somatic coaching for high-responsibility professionals navigating allostatic overload, decision fatigue, adrenal exhaustion, fight-or-flight vigilance that does not downshift, and depleted recovery capacity. The $3,800 container follows Bio-Audit™ and is calibrated to the client's current regulation state. It is the primary high-ticket pathway at Natoorales.
What is allostatic load and why does it matter?
Allostatic load is the cumulative physiological cost of adapting to chronic sustained stress. When allostatic load is high, recovery capacity degrades even when external pressure drops. This is why executives can rest and still not feel recovered — the autonomic regulatory system is still running on a dysregulated baseline. Natoorales works with allostatic load patterns within a coaching and wellness education scope.
What is vagal tone and why does it matter for executives?
Vagal tone refers to the activity of the vagus nerve — a primary regulator of the parasympathetic nervous system's rest-and-digest state. Low vagal tone is associated with poor stress recovery, emotional reactivity, sleep disruption, and difficulty returning to baseline. Somatic coaching supports vagal tone improvement within a wellness education and coaching scope.
Is this private 1:1 or a group program?
Every Natoorales engagement is private 1:1. Not a group course, cohort, or generic template. The work is calibrated to your actual capacity, constraints, pace, and current season of leadership or life.
Can this be done remotely?
Yes. Core services — including Bio-Audit™ and Executive Burnout Recovery — are available worldwide through structured video sessions. Ian Kain practices from Vancouver, Canada and Oaxaca, Mexico. Local in-person sessions are available in Huatulco by appointment.
Is Natoorales licensed clinical care?
No. Natoorales provides coaching, education, and wellness support only. It does not replace licensed clinical care, psychological care, or emergency support. If concerns are severe, acute, or urgent, contact qualified local professionals or emergency services immediately.
