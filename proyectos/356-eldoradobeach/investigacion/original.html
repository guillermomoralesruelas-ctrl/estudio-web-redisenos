About El Dorado

Live from Los Muertos Beach

World Class, Beach Front Dining

El Dorado has been a prime beachfront destination in Puerto Vallarta since the early 1960s.

Dine

Restaurant

Our open air concept features stunning woodwork and modern table settings, making it a unique venue to enjoy the outstanding seafood cuisine.

Relax

Beach Club

Our famous Beach Club is an amazing way to spend the day relaxing and enjoying the beautiful views and unique ambiance of Los Muertos Beach, a Puerto Vallarta favorite for over 60 years.

Celebrate

Groups & Events

El Dorado is the perfect venue for your special group or event, with spectacular service and a custom, tailor made experience, we will work with you to design the best night for your guests. Get in touch with our team today!
