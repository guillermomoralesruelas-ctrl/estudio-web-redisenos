Private Investment Advisory

Premium Real Estate Investment Advisory in Mexico

Selected properties, boutique hotels and private investment opportunities on the Oaxaca coast and in San Miguel de Allende.

Explore Selected Properties Hospitality Investments

For buyers who are not looking for the obvious Mexico.

Costa Dream Realty represents international buyers seeking selected real estate and hospitality assets in two distinct Mexican markets: the low-density Oaxaca coast and San Miguel de Allende. The work is private, deliberate and grounded in local market knowledge.

Selected markets and assets

Oaxaca Coast

Huatulco, Mazunte, Zipolite, Puerto Angel and Puerto Escondido. Selected homes, land and development opportunities across a coast defined by scarcity and character.

Explore Oaxaca Coast

Hospitality Assets

Boutique hotels, eco-lodges and operating hospitality assets for buyers evaluating both public opportunities and discreet off-market conversations.

View Hospitality Portfolio

San Miguel de Allende

A select heritage market for private homes and carefully chosen estate opportunities beyond Mexico's high-volume resort destinations.

Explore San Miguel

How we advise

Private representation

Focused advisory for buyers and owners who value discretion, preparation and a direct line of communication.

Selected opportunities

Residences, development land and hospitality assets evaluated for fit with the buyer's criteria and the realities of each market.

International perspective
