KITEBOARD CENTER

First kiteboarding school in the Riviera Maya. Since 2002.

Professional, experienced, and certified instructors. Lessons available in English, Spanish, German, Italian, and French.

· Wide range and size of kitesurf and wingfoil equipment for any wind condition and rider weight. 
· All lessons are supported by rescue boats. When needed, students and instructors are taken upwind by jet ski or boat, allowing more time for actual practice. (You learn 30–50% faster than in deep or wavy waters!) 
· We teach all ages, as long as students can swim. 
· We can train you from beginner level to very advanced tricks. 
· We have boards for kiteboard, kitesurf, kitefoil, wingfoil, and wing SUP. 
· We create custom packages for Groups and Families. Request a quote.

Fantastic place to have fun kitesurfing and relax! I spent one week at Ikarus to learn kitesurfing as a beginner. It was a blast!
