They were super knowledgeable and answered tons of questions we had. We got super lucky near the end of the tour and spotted a female, male, and baby all together jumping and splashing their fins and tails. It was incredible. A once in a lifetime sight. See the attached pics. | I would definitely ride out with Grimar again. Highly recommended.”

“This morning we did the Marietas Islands snorkeling tour with Grimar Adventures. The staff and crew were punctual and very courteous. Our crew, Ruben and Chuy, were both fantastic - very friendly and funny. While Ruben took the other passengers to Hidden Beach, Chuy boated us along the shore of the island so we could see all the birds. He was an amazing pilot, taking us into some narrow inlets for a very close look! And on the way back to town we spotted a family of humpback whales and they pulled in to allow us to watch them surface a few times. All in all an awesome excursion!”

David Mason
