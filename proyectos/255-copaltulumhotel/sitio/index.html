COPAL TULUM HOTEL



Copal Tulum Hotel is a boutique hotel crafted for modern travelers seeking a refined connection with nature. Each space has been intentionally designed to honor the surrounding environment, blending sustainable luxury with the raw beauty of the Mayan landscape. With a distinctive character and a deep sense of comfort, our wellness-inspired services and handpicked local experiences invite guests to unwind, reconnect, and feel completely at home. Whether you’re escaping for a quick retreat or embracing a longer stay,


Copal Tulum Hotel offers the ideal setting for restoration, inspiration, and tranquility.












THE LOCATION




Nestled within the lush tranquility of Aldea Zama, our hotel is set in one of Tulum’s most exclusive residential enclaves a refined sanctuary immersed in the jungle, yet effortlessly connected.     Just a short 10-minute drive from both the vibrant beach zone and the charming town center, our location offers the perfect balance between Tulum’s two worlds: the serenity of the jungle and the allure of the Caribbean Sea, the energy of its coastline and the authenticity of the pueblo.


Thoughtfully positioned for seamless access, it is an ideal starting point to explore, unwind, and experience Tulum from a place of calm sophistication.



Our amenities are designed to inspire: from restaurants, pools, lush gardens, a fully equipped gym, and holistic wellness spaces, to a rooftop with a jacuzzi, your very own private cenote, and more.

Everything you can imagine, all in one unforgettable place.gine.

Within & Beyond

WELLNESS & FITNESS


We invite you to explore a curated selection of authentic therapeutic experiences and natural treatments designed to awaken the senses, nourish the body, uplift the spirit, and reconnect you with your roots. Every element of our wellness center is thoughtfully crafted to inspire balance, renewal, and deep inner peace allowing you to fully embrace the healing energy of nature. In addition, for those who wish to maintain their active lifestyle, our fully equipped fitness center provides everything needed to continue your routine in perfect harmony with your stay.

DINING


Inspired by nature and guided by creativity, our two restaurants celebrate fresh local ingredients and vibrant flavor  from soulful Mexican cuisine to refined Japanese artistry.

Copal Gastro Bar -
Set within a lush jungle garden, Copal offers international cuisine with a Mexican soul, crafted from fresh, locally sourced ingredients and paired with signature mixology.

Kokoro -
An intimate rooftop experience devoted to Japanese precision and balance, featuring exceptional ingredients and elevated handcrafted cocktails.

EXPERIENCES


Thoughtfully curated for the inspired traveler, our experiences invite you to connect more deeply with nature, culture, and self. Rooted in the spirit of Tulum and its Mayan heritage, each moment is designed to feel intentional, immersive, and quietly transformative.

From ancestral temazcal ceremonies and hidden cenote explorations to sunrise yoga above the jungle canopy and intimate tastings of regional cuisine, our Guest Experience team curates journeys that honor the land and its traditions.
