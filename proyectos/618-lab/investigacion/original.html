Veracruz
from MX$390.00

Grown in the fertile coffee regions of Veracruz at elevations between 1,100 and 1,400 meters above sea level, this coffee showcases the unique character of the Oro Azteca variety. The Red Honey process preserves a portion of the fruit mucilage during drying, enhancing sweetness and complexity while maintaining a clean, balanced cup.

Cup Profile

  • Origin: Veracruz, Mexico

  • Process: Red Honey

  • Variety: Oro Azteca

  • Altitude: 1,100–1,400 MASL

  • Acidity: Malic

Tasting Notes
A smooth and sweet cup featuring rich chocolate notes, bright raspberry flavors, and a lingering caramel sweetness. The malic acidity adds a crisp, apple-like brightness that complements the coffee's creamy body and balanced finish.

Guerrero
from MX$390.00

Cultivated in the mountains of Guerrero at 1,800 meters above sea level, this coffee is produced from traditional Typica and Bourbon varieties. The natural process enhances its fruit-forward character, creating a vibrant and complex cup with remarkable sweetness and depth.

Cup Profile

  • Origin: Guerrero, Mexico

  • Process: Natural

  • Varieties: Typica, Bourbon

  • Altitude: 1,800 MASL

  • Acidity: Tartaric

Tasting Notes
A lively cup bursting with red fruits and blackberry notes, layered with rich cocoa undertones. The tartaric acidity brings a bright, wine-like character that enhances the coffee's sweetness and complexity, resulting in a smooth and memorable finish.
